Business outcome

Predictable Delivery

Centralized intake and measurable execution through Jira SLAs and cycle time tracking.

Result: reduced ambiguity, improved prioritization, and clearer workload visibility.

Business outcome

Scalable Onboarding Enablement

Full-stack digital asset lifecycle and LMS execution to support pilot readiness and scaling.

Result: consistent learner experience and faster iteration after pilot feedback.

Business outcome

Improved Learner Experience

Faster response to critical issues and a structured measurement loop (NPS/CSAT) tied to improvements.

Result: reduced support loops and increased usability of digital learning assets.

Role Scope (Owned Responsibilities)

Full-stack L&D
  • Content Production: Articulate, Canva, Synthesia; SCORM packaging; module QA.
  • LMS Administration: courses/modules, cohorts/groups, assignments, programs/paths, certificates, reporting.
  • Learner Support (Tier 1): LMS login issues, reassignment, completion recovery, troubleshooting.

Operational Boundaries (to maintain fair KPIs)

Governance
  • System-of-record: work is tracked and measured when it enters Jira.
  • IT boundary: HD network/SSO issues route to IT unless explicitly defined otherwise.
  • Approval dependency: content timelines depend on SME review turnaround (tracked as a status).
