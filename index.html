Why Jira matters for MBOs

It is the definitive evidence layer for "On-time Learning Operations Execution" and removes subjectivity from workload evaluation.

Measurable: Accurately tracks SLA % and backlog age.
Scalable: Onboarding growth won't break operations.
Protects Scope: Untracked work remains "invisible" otherwise.
